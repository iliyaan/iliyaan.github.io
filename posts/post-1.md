---
title: Starting this site
date: August 2026
excerpt: Why I'm building this, and what I plan to write about here.
---

I've wanted a simple place to write for a while — somewhere between a lab
notebook and a personal journal. No comment sections, no algorithm deciding
who sees it, just a clean page I control.

The plan is to post here when something's worth writing down: a research
idea that finally clicked, something I built, or a lesson from working on
the business side of things.
